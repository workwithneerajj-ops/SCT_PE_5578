🚗 **ParkEasy – Product Management Case Study**

🅿️ ParkEasy is a smart parking product designed to simplify parking discovery, reservation, navigation, and digital payment.

🎯 **Project Objective**

ParkEasy aims to make urban parking faster, simpler, and more predictable for everyday drivers.

The project explores the complete Product Management lifecycle, from understanding users to defining and planning the final product.

👤**Task 1 – User Persona & Empathy Map**
